Getting good results

  • Use the sharpest image you can. In Nastaliq, letters sit close together on a slanted baseline, so resolution matters more than for other scripts.
  • Crop to one column of text. Newspaper pages with many columns work better one column at a time.
  • Photograph the page flat and straight on, so the lines are level.
  • Check names and rare words: they are where OCR makes most mistakes.

Questions

Why is Urdu OCR harder than Arabic OCR?

Most Urdu print uses Nastaliq, where each word slopes down from right to left and letters overlap. Most OCR systems were built for Naskh, the flatter style used for Arabic, and read Nastaliq poorly. That's one of the gaps we want to close.

Does it work for Urdu newspapers?

Yes, but crop one column or article at a time. A whole newspaper page is too much for one image.

Can I paste the result into Word or InPage?

The result is Unicode text, so you can paste it into Word, Google Docs or any Unicode editor. InPage uses its own format, so paste into a Unicode editor first.

Is Urdu handwriting supported?

Neat handwriting sometimes works, but results vary a lot. Handwriting is one of the things our own model, Rune-1, is being trained for.
