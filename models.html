Models

Rune-1

Our first OCR model family. Rune-1 finds the text regions on a page, works out the reading order, recognises each line, printed or handwritten, and returns Markdown, JSON or a searchable PDF.

In training Until it's ready, you can try our preview models for free.

One architecture, three versions.

Rune-1 Small is open and runs on your own hardware. Rune-1 is the larger model behind the API. Rune-1 Custom is trained for a particular script or collection.

Rune-1 Custom

Talk to us

Scripts that most OCR handles badly.

Most OCR is tuned for Latin text in neat columns. We train Rune-1 on right-to-left, vertical and mixed-script pages from the start: Arabic, Persian (Farsi) and Urdu, including nastaliq; Hindi, Marathi and Nepali in Devanagari; Amharic and Tigrinya in Ge'ez; Tibetan; Chinese and Japanese; and historical manuscripts through handwritten text recognition (HTR).

We'll publish coverage for each script, marked production, beta or research, so you know what to expect before you send a page.

In the cloud, on your servers or on a device.

Use the API, run the model inside your own network, or ship it in an app. The output format is the same in all three.

RuneBench

An open benchmark for OCR on low-resource scripts. We report character error rate for each script separately instead of one average. The dataset, the scoring code and all prediction files will be public, including the pages where we do badly. We'll also report results on established public benchmarks, so Rune can be compared with other systems.
