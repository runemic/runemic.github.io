Rune-1 comes in three versions: Small, Rune-1 and Custom.

      
Rune-1 Small is open and runs on your own hardware. Rune-1 is the larger model behind the API. Rune-1 Custom is trained for a particular script or collection.

Arabic, Devanagari, CJK and more: scripts that most OCR handles badly.

      
Most OCR is tuned for Latin text in neat columns. We train Rune-1 on right-to-left, vertical and mixed-script pages from the start: Arabic, Persian (Farsi) and Urdu, including nastaliq; Hindi, Marathi and Nepali in Devanagari; Amharic and Tigrinya in Ge'ez; Tibetan; Chinese and Japanese; and historical manuscripts through handwritten text recognition (HTR).

      
We'll publish coverage for each script, marked production, beta or research, so you know what to expect before you send a page.

    

    
  


  

    

      
Run Rune-1 in the cloud, on your servers or on a device.

      
Use the API, run the model inside your own network, or ship it in an app. The output format is the same in all three.

    

    

      
Cloud API
Send pages to api.runemic.com and get the text back. Nothing to install.

      
Your servers
Run Rune-1 inside your own network, so documents never leave it.

      
On a device
Rune-1 Small runs on a laptop or in an app, even offline.

    

  


  

    

      

        
RuneBench: an open OCR benchmark for low-resource scripts

        
An open benchmark for OCR on low-resource scripts. We report character error rate for each script separately instead of one average. The dataset, the scoring code and every engine's output are public, including the pages where we do badly. We'll also report results on established public benchmarks, so Rune can be compared with other systems.

        
Public: Persian
