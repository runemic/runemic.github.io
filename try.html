Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can read 5 pages a day here without an account. If you sign in, you get $5 of free credit, which is about 2,500 pages at preview prices (up to 50 on your first day, then up to 500 a day). Nothing is billed during the preview.

Which languages and scripts does it read?

Most widely used scripts, including Latin, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew, Cyrillic, Greek, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Results depend on the image: a sharp, straight photo works best. Rune-1, our own model, is being trained for the harder cases.

Do you keep my images?

No. The image is sent to the model, the text comes back to you, and nothing is saved. We only count how many pages you've used today.

I'm signed in. Does this page use my credit?

No. This page always uses the 5 free pages a day, even when you are signed in. Your credit is used in the playground and through the API: up to 500 pages a day, or 50 on the first day of a new account.

Can I use it from my own code?

Yes. Sign in to the console, create an API key and send images to POST /v1/ocr. See the API docs.

Can it read PDFs?

Not yet. For now, take a screenshot of the page or export it as an image. PDF support comes with Rune-1.
