Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You can read 10 pages a day here without an account. If you sign in, you get 2,500 free credits. The preview models cost 2–3 credits a page, so that is up to 1,250 pages (up to 50 on your first day, then up to 500 a day). Nothing is billed during the preview.

Which languages and scripts does it read?

Most widely used scripts, including Latin, Arabic, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew, Cyrillic, Greek, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Results depend on the image: a sharp, straight photo works best. Rune-1, our own model, is being trained for the harder cases.

Do you keep my images?

No. The image is sent to the model, the text comes back to you, and nothing is saved. We only count how many pages you've used today.

I'm signed in. Does this page use my credits?

Yes, once you sign in on this page: each page is then read with your credits instead of the free pages (2–3 credits a page with the preview models). Not signed in, the page uses the 10 free pages a day.

Can I use it from my own code?

Yes. Sign in to the console, create an API key and send images to POST /v1/ocr. See the API docs, or code examples in eight languages.

Can it read PDFs?

Not on this page. PDF to text reads every page of a PDF: pages that already contain text are read from the file, and scanned pages are read with OCR.

Can it translate the text?

Yes: Translate an image reads the image the same way and translates the text into 21 languages, with the text that was read shown beside the translation.

Can I get a table as an Excel file?

Yes: Image to Excel reads the image the same way and gives you an .xlsx or CSV file. More free tools are on tools.runemic.com.
