About

Most of the world's writing is still on paper.

Letters, ledgers, manuscripts, receipts, whole libraries. Most of it can't be searched, and the scripts it is written in are the ones technology serves worst. Runemic exists to change that, for every script and not just the ones with the most data.

The name

Rune + Numeric

Rune: symbols, writing, ancient scripts, deciphering text. Numeric: data, numbers, computation, machines. Runemic is the work in between: turning written symbols into structured digital information.

Our mark is the rune ᚱ, the letter R, drawn inside a scanner's frame. Its corners are data points, and the line crossing it is the scan.

Roadmap

Software first, then the machines.

We start with the models, because everything else depends on them. Then the products people use every day, then the services and hardware to digitize whole collections.

Principles

How we work

Open by default

We publish model weights, benchmarks and evaluation code. You should be able to check our claims yourself.

Measured honestly

Accuracy is reported per script and per document type in RuneBench, including where we are weak.

Private by design

Your documents are yours. We don't train on them, and we offer ways to run everything on your own machines.

Work with us

Building something with documents, sitting on an archive, or want to help digitize the world's writing? We'd like to hear from you.
