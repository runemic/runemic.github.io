Getting good results

  • Photograph the page straight on, in good light, so the lines are level.
  • Printed text in Naskh-style fonts works best. Nastaliq calligraphy and handwriting are harder, so check those results carefully.
  • Pages that mix Persian and English are fine: each line keeps its own direction.
  • Crop away the edges of the book or the desk; the less background, the better.

Questions

Is this different from Arabic OCR?

Persian has four letters Arabic doesn't (پ چ ژ گ) and uses its own forms of ی and ک. The model is asked to copy the text exactly as written, so these should come back as Persian letters. If your page mixes Persian and Arabic, check the result.

Does it read Persian digits?

Yes. Persian digits (۱۲۳) and Western digits (123) are both read and returned as they appear on the page.

How do I paste the text into Word or Google Docs?

Copy it or download it as a file, then paste it. If your editor doesn't switch to right-to-left by itself, set the paragraph direction to right-to-left.
